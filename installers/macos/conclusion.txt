Installation complete.

Open Word, Excel, or PowerPoint, then click:

    Insert > Add-ins  (or Insert > My Add-ins on older Office builds)

Switch to the "Developer Add-ins" tab. Title Capitalizer will appear
there. Click it to load the add-in - a "Title Capitalizer" group will
appear on the Home tab.

To remove the add-in, delete the file "manifest.xml" from each of:

  ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Word/Data/Documents/wef/
  ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Excel/Data/Documents/wef/
  ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Powerpoint/Data/Documents/wef/
